# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## The short version

- Be kind and assume good intent. People here range from first-time contributors to professional musicians and engineers.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualized content, trolling, insults, doxxing, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Do not share recordings of other people made with VIVO without their consent.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact form at [alamedinsabit.me](https://alamedinsabit.me). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
