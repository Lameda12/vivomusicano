# Safety notice

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Please read this before you play.

## Photosensitivity

Vivomusicano shows bright flashes when a chord changes. A small share of people have seizures triggered by flashing lights or patterns, sometimes with no prior history. If you or anyone in your family has epilepsy or has had a seizure, talk to a doctor before using Vivomusicano. **Stop right away** if you feel dizzy, have blurred vision, eye or muscle twitches, or feel disoriented.

## Hearing

Synth pads can get loud fast, and raising your left hand raises the volume. Set your system volume low before you press START, then turn it up slowly. Avoid long sessions at high volume on headphones.

## Physical space and strain

You'll be moving your arms in front of a camera. Clear the space around you, keep away from screens, lamps, and other people, and take breaks to avoid arm and shoulder strain.

## Camera and people around you

The camera image is shown on screen. Be aware of what else is in frame, especially if you are streaming or sharing your screen.

## Live performance

Hand tracking can miss or misread a gesture in bad lighting or with fast movement. For a live show, keep the keyboard fallback (`Space` / `→`) in reach and test your setup in the venue lighting first.
