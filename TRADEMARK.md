# Trademark policy

The code is MIT-licensed, so you are free to fork it, change it, and ship it. The **Vivomusicano** name and any Vivomusicano logo are not covered by that license; they identify the official project and the upcoming official apps.

## You may

- say your project is "based on Vivomusicano" or "a fork of Vivomusicano",
- use the name to refer to the project in articles, videos, talks, and tutorials,
- keep the name in an unmodified, non-commercial deployment.

## Please don't

- name a modified version, app, or product "Vivomusicano" or something confusingly similar,
- publish an app in an app store under the Vivomusicano name,
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests we endorse you.

Want to use the name for something else? Ask through [alamedinsabit.me](https://alamedinsabit.me).
